# Dungeons

[Home](../README.md) · [Getting started](getting-started.md) · [Start screen](start-screen.md) · [Main screen](main-screen.md) · **Dungeons** · [Hotkeys](hotkeys.md) · [Options](options.md)

Each dungeon has its own tab (1 to 9), and the **S** tab shows a summary of all of them. **1Q** and **2Q** switch between the first and second quest layouts.

## Marking rooms

Click a room to open the room menu. Pick the room type (stairs, shop, old man, boss and so on), what it holds, and which doors are open, locked, bombable or walled. Right-click and middle-click give more choices, and the room menu can also be driven by [hotkeys](hotkeys.md).

- **Doors** sit between rooms. The red **no** wall marks a door you cannot pass.
- **Old Man**, **Bomb Upgrade** and **Princess** rooms place their usual wall for you. Changing the room to another type takes that wall back off.
- **OFF rooms** are rooms that are not part of the dungeon. Marking a room OFF clears any manual no-wall against it, and (with **Red Edge By OFF Tiles** on, the default) draws a red edge where the dungeon meets it.

## Dungeon summary

The **S** tab shows every dungeon at once. Hover a dungeon board on the main screen to preview its tab, and click to switch.

## Clear a dungeon

Right-click a dungeon's number in the tab row to clear its whole map (rooms, doors, circles and icons). You are asked to confirm first. Use it if you marked the wrong dungeon.

To reset a single room back to a brand new one, press **mouse 4** over it. Hold mouse 4 and slide over more rooms to reset each one.

## Item boxes and blockers

Each dungeon's item row sits at the top of the main screen. Mark what you found there, and note anything blocking a dungeon in the **Blockers** boxes.

## Settings

Dungeon behavior (hint icons, BOARD instead of LEVEL, basement info, door inference, the red OFF edges and more) is under **Dungeon Settings** in [Options](options.md).
