# Options

[Home](../README.md) · [Getting started](getting-started.md) · [Start screen](start-screen.md) · [Main screen](main-screen.md) · [Dungeons](dungeons.md) · [Hotkeys](hotkeys.md) · **Options**

Open **Options** on the start screen or the main screen. Most settings take effect immediately and are remembered.

| Section | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Overworld Settings** | Route drawing, highlighting nearby tiles, the magnifier and popup style. |
| **Dungeon Settings** | Hint icons, BOARD instead of LEVEL, basement info, door inference, **Red Edge By OFF Tiles** and more. |
| **Reminders** | Volume, which voice and visual reminders you get (dungeon feedback, sword hearts, coast item, a check of Overworld + Extras when an option is Random and more), how often they repeat, and which voice speaks them. |
| **Auto-save** | How often the tracker saves your run, in minutes. |
| **Other** | Animations, remembering your startup choices, update checks, Display Seed + Flags, listening for speech, the broadcast window, the timer and Race Mode. |
| **Co-Op** | Client and Host settings for tracking together with a partner. |
| **Show Crash Log...** | Opens the crash log to attach when reporting a problem. |

**Guided Tour** on the start screen walks through all of this.
