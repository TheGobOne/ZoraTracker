# ZORATracker 3.3.1 release notes (draft)

Compared against the published 3.3 release. Not published.

## Discord copy

**ZORATracker 3.3.1**

**New**
- Shuffle Type Anywhere: item boxes now take maps, compasses and shuffled Triforce Pieces from the item menu, then ask which level it is for. The Triforce hotkey does the same. Triforce Piece, map and compass have their own row in the item menu.

**Changes**
- All item boxes can now place a Triforce, Map, or Compass.
- Item menu: the clear cell is now a red X.
- Intra Heart Shuffle: a dungeon's last open slot now fills with a heart once the other slots have items, collected or not.
- Go time: with all triforces, the bow and silver arrows in hand, the reminder now says you are go time, even without the ladder or recorder.

**Fixes**
- The item box beside the sword now shows its level tag.
- The same level's Triforce Piece, map or compass can no longer be picked for two boxes.
- M/C/Tri Shuffle window: no longer asks for a level on a Triforce Piece when triforces are not shuffled.
- Guided Tour: button text stays visible on hover, and the text about menus and basement triforces is corrected.

Thanks to everyone for the bug reports and feedback.
